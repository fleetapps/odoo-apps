- The `per_week` basis counts **whole completed weeks**. A clause reading "per
  week or part thereof" is different arithmetic; record it as a daily rate
  rather than having this round up, because guessing costs the counterparty
  money.
- A charge is deducted in the agreement's own currency. Where the invoice is in
  another currency the wizard refuses rather than converting, since the rate to
  apply to a contractual figure is a commercial question.
- No demo data ships. It would have to reference an expense account, and
  accounts are created per company by the chart template.
