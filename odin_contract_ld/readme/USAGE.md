**Record the clause.** Accounting → Liquidated Damages → Agreements. Enter the
contract value, the rate, the cap and the contractual completion date, then
Activate. Damages accrue from the day after completion (plus any grace period)
and stop at the cap.

**Claim an extension.** Add an Extension of Time on the agreement. Only an
*approved* claim moves the completion date — submitting one changes nothing,
which is the point.

**Raise a charge.** *Raise Charge* creates a charge for everything accrued but
not yet charged. Assess it, have a manager approve it, then *Deduct on Document*
to add it as a negative line on a draft invoice or bill. The counterparty sees
the deduction on the document they are paying against, and the Damages
Assessment PDF shows the working behind it.

**Waive rather than delete.** A concession is recorded with its reason and stays
on the record. Deleting a charge to reflect a commercial deal leaves the file
looking like an error.

**Close.** Set the actual completion date, mark the agreement Completed, settle
or waive every open charge, then Close.
