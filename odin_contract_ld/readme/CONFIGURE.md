1. Give the users who assess damages the *Liquidated Damages / User* group, and
   whoever approves or waives them *Liquidated Damages / Manager*. Assessing and
   forgiving are deliberately different authorities.
2. Create (or pick) the accounts the deduction posts to:
   - an **expense** account for damages the employer levies on you;
   - an **other income** account for damages you recover from subcontractors.
3. The daily *Liquidated Damages: accrue and alert* scheduled action keeps
   running agreements current and raises an activity at 75% and 100% of the cap.
   It is enabled on install.
