**Budget the project.** Accounting → Cost Control → Budgets. One line per
workstream, each pointing at its analytic account. Confirm it — a draft budget
is a proposal and stays out of the cost control report.

**Commitments look after themselves.** Confirming a purchase order creates one
commitment per analytic account on each line, pro-rated by the distribution. The
amounts are computed from the order line, so an order that changes after
confirmation keeps its commitment right without anything re-running.

**Record what has no order yet.** A signed subcontract that has not become a
purchase order is still money promised. Add it as a manual commitment.

**Read the position.** The budget shows budget, committed, actual, consumed and
remaining per workstream, with an overrun flag. Cost Control Analysis pivots all
three legs together, grouped however you like. The PDF gives the same by
workstream with the definitions on it, so a reader knows what they are looking
at.

**Revise, don't edit.** *Create Revision* supersedes a confirmed budget with a
new draft and keeps the original readable — it is what the earlier cost reports
were run against.
