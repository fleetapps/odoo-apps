# odoo-apps
Odoo apps and modules for all businesses

| Module | Odoo | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [`odin_bar_base`](odin_bar_base) | 19.0 | One definition of a bar: location, operation types, analytic account, POS mapping, trading days |
| [`odin_bar_desk`](odin_bar_desk) | 19.0 | Bar Desk: phone-first stock outs, blind counts with manager approval, delivery checks and store mode |
| [`gymkhana_pos_import`](gymkhana_pos_import) | 19.0 | Post a day of bar sales from the POS Group Sales Register PDF: sale orders, bar deliveries and one invoice, recorded per bar for the counts |
| [`odin_account_pnl`](odin_account_pnl) | 19.0 | The Profit & Loss of Odoo Enterprise for Community, on one page: unfold in place, split any figure by partner, invoice, purchase or sales order, product, day to quarter or analytic, peek at documents, inline budgets, lines / accounts / every transaction on screen and in PDF/XLSX, saved views emailed on a schedule |
| [`odin_account_ai`](odin_account_ai) | 19.0 | Accounting > AI, on Claude: Ask the Ledger with clickable evidence, Explain any P&L change, Transaction Review of bank lines and draft bills, nightly Anomalies, and journal entries drafted from a sentence. Nothing is booked without an accountant |
| [`odin_client_quote`](odin_client_quote) | 19.0 | Cover Quotes: client quotation and side-by-side cover comparison PDFs written for the person paying the premium, shared on WhatsApp |
| [`odin_medical_quote`](odin_medical_quote) | 19.0 | Add Medical Quote: who is covered, every plan in the client's budget priced for that family, the chosen plans side by side with discount, levies and total; saved to Contacts and the CRM pipeline, and confirmed and invoiced when the client accepts |
| [`odin_bizsure_quotes`](odin_bizsure_quotes) | 19.0 | The BizSure rate cards as data: APA Jamii Plus and Birdview Bizsure Afya products, levies, insurers and 16 quotation templates |
| [`whatsapp_connector`](whatsapp_connector) | 19.0 | One company WhatsApp Business number for many Odoo users: Enterprise-style WhatsApp conversations in Discuss, or conversations routed to salespeople as CRM leads. Specification: [SPEC.md](whatsapp_connector/SPEC.md) |
