1. Give the trade-finance team *Letters of Credit / User*, and whoever issues,
   closes and accepts presentations *Letters of Credit / Manager*.
2. Review **Accounting → Letters of Credit → Document Types**. The usual UCP 600
   set is seeded; add whatever your trade lanes call for.
3. The daily *Letters of Credit: expiry and shipment watch* scheduled action
   expires lapsed credits and raises an activity 30 days before expiry and 14
   days before the latest shipment date. It is enabled on install.
