Documentary credits, and the one thing they exist to do: release money only
against a complete set of documents.

Standard Odoo has no letter of credit at all. Businesses that live on them keep
the register in a spreadsheet, tick documents off on paper, and discover an
expiry the week after it passed. The expensive failure is always the same shape
— the goods shipped, the credit lapsed, and what was a bank's undertaking became
a conversation.

The module holds the credit, its document set, its amendments, its drawings and
the bank charges against it. A bill settled under a credit cannot be paid while
any required document is outstanding: the hold is enforced where the money
actually moves, in the payment register, not as a note somebody may read.

Both sides are modelled. An import credit is one we applied for in a supplier's
favour; an export credit is one opened in ours. Project exposure counts the
undrawn availability on live import credits — money committed but not yet paid.
