- Presentation period defaults to 21 days, the UCP 600 default where the credit
  is silent. It is per-credit and should be set from the credit's own wording.
- Bank charges are recorded against the credit and carry an analytic
  distribution, but are not posted automatically — the bank's debit advice is
  the source document and should be entered as a bill.
- A revolving credit is modelled as a type but the reinstatement cycle is not
  automated; record each cycle's drawings and amend the amount.
- No SWIFT (MT700/MT707) import. Everything is keyed from the advice.
